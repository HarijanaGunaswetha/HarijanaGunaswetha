Transportation

Bengaluru Smart City integrates public transit, e-mobility, and digital infrastructure into a connected, multi-modal network.

Namma Metro:

The rapid transit system features expanding Purple and Green lines. You can seamlessly recharge smart cards, plan trips, and book metro tickets through integrated digital platforms.

Electric Buses (BMTC):

Bengaluru’s bus network includes hundreds of modern, low-floor electric buses equipped with real-time tracking, CCTV, and panic buttons. You can track buses and view estimated arrival times using the official BMTC App.

Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS) Apps:

The city operates integrated, digital MaaS applications that allow you to plan routes, book end-to-end travel (metro, buses, and auto-rickshaws), and pay via a unified, single payment gateway.

First & Last-Mile Connectivity:

To solve the daily commute, you can rent dockless e-bikes and bicycles located near major transit hubs. App-based cab aggregators and auto-rickshaws are also integrated directly into the city's smart mobility apps.

Smart Transit Hubs:

Integrated mobility hubs (like the one at Shivaji Nagar) and digitized bus stops feature digital displays with real-time traffic updates, route planning kiosks, and passenger safety

Traffic Rules And Fines

Traffic Rules & FinesMobile Phones:

Using a handheld mobile phone while driving or riding is an offense.
Fines range from \(\text{₹}1,500\) to \(\text{₹}10,000\), though phones may be used strictly for navigation purposes.

Traffic Rules & FinesMobile Phones:

Disobeying one-way streets attracts a \(\text{₹}500\) penalty, and crossing or
stopping at a pedestrian stop line carries a \(\text{₹}1,000\) fine.

Drunk Driving & Documents:

Driving under the influence of alcohol, or operating a vehicle without valid insurance,
fitness certificates, and a driving license, leads to severe penalties, court fines, or vehicle impoundment.
